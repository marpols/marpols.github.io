I am a PhD Student in the [Water Resource and Environmental Systems Lab](https://www.water-environment.lab.mcgill.ca/) under the co-supervision of [Dr. Zhiming Qi](https://www.mcgill.ca/bioeng/qi-zhiming) of McGill University and [Dr. Morteza Mesbah](https://profils-profiles.science.gc.ca/en/profile/morteza-mesbah) of Agriculture and Agri-food Canada. I previously completed a B. Sc. (Agr.) at the [University of Guelph](https://www.plant.uoguelph.ca/) specializing in plant agriculture and a B. Sc at the [University of Toronto](https://web.cs.toronto.edu/) where I double majored in computer science and linguistics. 

My PhD research explores the intersection of process based crop models and machine learning to obtain agricultural insights and create decision support tools, with a current focus on applying these technologies to potato production in [Prince Edward Island](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FPmQFyeRn8EQUYPfA), Canada. I also have experience in controlled environment agriculture working with [CESRF](https://www.plant.uoguelph.ca/people-and-places/research-centres/cesrf-controlled-environment-systems-research-facility) at the University of Guelph for my undergraduate bachelor's thesis as well as in plant pathology and pest control while working with the Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Agribusiness. 

In my free time I enjoy cycling, cooking, and [knitting](https://www.ravelry.com/people/marpopo). You can reach me best by email through mariaelisa.polsinelli[at]mail[dot]mcgill[dot]ca or mariaelisa.polsinelli[at]agr[dot]gc[dot]ca. I'm always happy to chat and connect!
